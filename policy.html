Cabin Baggage Policy — Season Winter 2026/2027

Updated: October 15, 2026 — This policy applies to all flights departing from October 27, 2026 onwards.

Cabin Baggage Allowance by Class

Travel Class Number of Bags Max. Weight per Bag Personal Item
Economy Light 1 8 kg 1 small bag (max. 4 kg)
Economy Standard 1 10 kg 1 small bag (max. 4 kg)
Premium Economy 1 12 kg 1 small bag (max. 5 kg)
Business Class 2 14 kg each 1 laptop bag (max. 5 kg)
First Class 2 18 kg each 1 laptop bag (max. 6 kg)
Maximum Dimensions: All cabin bags must fit within 56 × 40 × 25 cm (including handles and wheels). Personal items must fit under the seat in front of you (max. 40 × 30 × 15 cm).

Special Items

  • Musical instruments: Accepted as cabin baggage if they fit in the overhead bin. Larger instruments require an extra seat purchase.
  • Duty-free purchases: Permitted in addition to your cabin allowance when sealed in a STEB bag with a valid receipt.
  • Assistive devices: Wheelchairs, canes, and medical devices are carried free of charge and do not count toward your allowance.
  • Infant items: One stroller or car seat per infant, gate-checked at no extra cost.

Prohibited Items in Cabin

  • Liquids, aerosols, and gels exceeding 100 ml per container (must fit in a 1-litre transparent bag)
  • Sharp objects (scissors with blades over 6 cm, box cutters, knives)
  • Lithium batteries exceeding 160 Wh (spare batteries must be in carry-on, never checked)
  • Self-heating meals and beverages

Excess Baggage Fees

Route Type Per Extra Kg (at gate) Per Extra Kg (pre-booked online)
Domestic €12 €8
Short-haul International €15 €10
Long-haul International €18 €13

Note: SkyBridge Airlines reserves the right to weigh cabin bags at the gate. Bags exceeding the allowance may be checked at the applicable excess fee. Frequent Flyer Gold and Platinum members receive an additional 5 kg cabin allowance on all classes. Policy subject to change; always verify at skybridgeairlines.example.com/baggage before travelling.
